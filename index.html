FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Will you take my idea?

No. Your idea stays yours. We're in this to build companies with founders, not around them.

Do I have to give up equity?

No. Community events, service packages and network partnerships involve no equity. A full partnership includes capital and a stake, and the capital comes in steps as you hit milestones.

I already run a profitable business. Is this for me?

Yes. Many of the owners we want to meet already have customers and revenue but aren't sure how to scale. Start with a free Scale Check.

Do I need to be in St. Louis?

No. We're based in St. Louis, but most sessions are online and we work with owners across the Midwest.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Scale Nights, Founder Stories and the Scale Check are free.

What kinds of businesses fit best?

B2B services where technology, a strong sales system and the right introductions can change how fast you grow.
