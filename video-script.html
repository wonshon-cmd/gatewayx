GatewayX · Video script

About 6 minutes. Each person presents their own chapter of the paper, in order.

Open the presentationPractice with notesAuto-play for recording

How to record

  1. Practice: open Practice with notes. Your lines show at the bottom of every slide. Press N to hide or show them, and → for the next slide.
  2. Record the slides once: open Auto-play for recording on a laptop in full screen (F11 / Ctrl+Cmd+F), and screen-record it. It moves on by itself using the timing below (about 6:10). Press P to pause.
  3. Record your voice and face: each person records their chapter on their phone or laptop camera (a short clip, landscape). Read your lines from the table below.
  4. Put it together: drop the slide recording and everyone's clips into one editor (Descript, CapCut, iMovie or Canva) and line up each clip with its chapter. Put the speaker in a small bubble in the corner of the slides.
