Perspective-taking · founders who understand investors

Sell to the investor's real pain

📈
What VCs need
  • Power-law outcomes: a few home runs return the fund
  • So they push speed, scale and follow-on rounds
💧
What GatewayX's LPs needed
  • Stakes in many funds, little cash back in a decade
  • GatewayX offered quarterly cash distributions from profits: “like a hedge”

“You wanted to raise a fund. That's selling. It's not investing.”

Strebulaev & Dang (HBR, 2024); interview with Adam Brakhane and Shonak Patel

Culture, strategy and purpose · the tension

The equity stake vs. “founders first”

First $500K check10%
Automatic second check at $500K revenue + $25K profit~20%
GatewayX's view
  • Founders keep ~80% at exit, vs. ~40% on a typical venture path
Our view
  • Only if the company reaches profit
  • Offer flexible financing: preferred + common, or equity + debt

Recommendation · four moves

Four moves, matched to what founders want

1. Showcase sales and marketing strengthsGrowthAssistant and Aux Insights, whose AI-native delivery cut costs 60–70%.
